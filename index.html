From signals to engineering solutions · 전체 학습 경로

Start from signals and LTI systems, learn frequency representations, generalize to complex frequency and ROC, then connect continuous signals to digital processing. The application collection will use the full sequence to solve real problems.

01 · FoundationsCh 1–2 · Signals, systems, impulse response, convolution
02 · RepresentationsCh 3–4 · Fourier series and CTFT: periodic vs nonperiodic
03 · Systems & ROCCh 5 · Laplace: poles, ROC, causality, stability
04 · Digital processingCh 6–8 · DTFT, sampling, Z-transform, DFT/FFT tools

Key bridge: Chapter 2 introduces y = x*h ↔ Y = HX for LTI systems when the relevant transforms exist. Chapter 7 connects continuous and discrete time through sampling.

Applications · planned extension

Audio & speech · Images & video · Sensor/IoT signals · Communications · Control. Each case will follow Problem → Theory → Process → Results → Reflection, rather than the four-step introductory experiment format.
